01 / What You'll Need

Four things, and you're ready to start.

  • Claude Code. The desktop app is easiest — no terminal required. The CLI and IDE extension work identically.Don't have it? See the "No Claude Code" chapter at the end.

03 / Skill One — Setup

Run once, before either build skill. It refuses to build from nothing, and it never makes you re-explain what it can already find.

1

It looks before it asks

Claude reads your business folder first — about docs, past emails, captions, a CLAUDE.md, prior work — and reports back what it already pieced together, marked confident, guessing, or missing.

2

It interviews only the gaps

One question at a time, never a form, across three passes: your voice, a starting sketch of your ideal client, and your current offer.

3

It drafts four calibration files

about-me.md, voice-rules.md, ideal-client.md, and my-offer.md, saved into an ABOUT ME/ folder. You see every file before it's saved — your edit always wins.

4

It ends with proof, not a folder

A short dress rehearsal: it writes just the opening of a class in your voice and asks — does this sound like you? — before handing you to Build ICP.

04 / Skill Two — Build ICP

Turns your sketch into one real human, felt so vividly you could write straight to them.

1

The interview

One question at a time about the business, the offer, and the transformation you help someone make — then deeper: a real Tuesday, what exhausts them, what they secretly want, what they're afraid of, what they've already tried and why it failed, the exact words in their own head.

2

The mirror move

In personal, relationship-driven businesses, the ideal client is often a version of the person being interviewed.

Worth knowingIf answers keep coming out generic, the questions turn on you directly — your own honest pains and fears are usually the truest picture of your audience.
3

Real research

Once the interview's done, Claude looks for real people who match — Reddit, reviews, forums, comments — and quotes their actual language, not a paraphrase.

4

The dossier

Pains, fears, desires, and objections — every one written as the feeling underneath, never the behavior. Ranked top 10 each, plus 10 ad hooks, 10 content ideas, 5 class titles, and a handoff block Build Masterclass reads automatically.

05 / Skill Three — Build Masterclass

The governing law: never sell the thing. Sell the emotion underneath it.

1

Feed it the finished ICP

The handoff block Build ICP ends with, or a hand-written one shaped the same way.

2

A short presenter intake

Your before-story and turning point, your proof, the one big promise, the three discoveries you'll teach, and the offer & CTA as they stand today.

3

Phase 1 — the full script

The complete spoken class, start to finish, built on the fixed 17-part spine.

4

Phase 2 — the slide map

A numbered, slide-by-slide breakdown separating the on-screen line from what you actually say.

5

Phase 3 — optional Canva build

On request, Claude builds the actual deck from the Phase 2 map. The slide map alone is a complete, human-buildable deliverable.
